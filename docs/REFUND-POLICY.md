# Refund Policy

> **Status: concept stage — no money is charged today.** Last updated 11 October 2026.

## Now

There is nothing to pay on this site at concept stage. No deposits, no fees, no purchases exist. Accordingly, there is nothing to refund — and nothing to be charged for.

## When payments begin (draft rules, published in advance)

Refunds are designed into the campaign before any paid transaction is ever possible:

1. **Bidder deposit (₹2,000, planned):** fully refundable — automatically if you are outbid, and if you withdraw before close under the published auction rules.
2. **Full-livery-or-refund:** if any lot is unsold at close, the auction voids and **every payment and every deposit is returned in full**. No partial campaign ever ships.
3. **30/70 split:** only 30% is due on award; the remaining 70% is due only after your install photography is delivered — fail that, and the balance is refunded under the signed agreement.
4. **Rail:** all refunds return through the same payment gateway the money arrived on — never to a different account, never in kind.

These mechanics are **draft** and take effect only after a payment provider approves the flows and the final auction rules are published. They will be published here with dates before any money can move.
