# Chrome Yatra — The 24-Surface Study (recovered archive)

The original 24-surface inventory from the campaign's first model — deleted
from the app inventory in an operator error, recovered in full from git
history (commit 92194c81) and from the app database, and preserved here as
a research source. Nothing is lost. These 24 records are preserved here — in this repository's own history,
the campaign's durable, vendor-free home.

## Ideas extracted (why this archive matters)

1. **Granular surface thinking.** The 24 model mapped surfaces the current
   five-lot machine livery doesn't sell: jacket sleeves, crash guard plates, swingarm
   tubes, flyscreen, front cowl, individual tank top center. This is the
   machine's full decal-able real estate map — the master reference for any
   future expansion.
2. **Placement authority as a product.** The old fork-leg lots were
   "PLACEMENT AUTHORITY ONLY" — the buyer decides what goes there, rather
   than their own decal. A genuinely unusual idea worth reusing: an
   "Authority lot" where a brand curates the space, not just occupies it.
3. **The Complete Livery Buyout.** A future 13th meta-lot: every unsold
   micro-surface from this map bundled as one premium "full coverage"
   package for a single deep-pocketed sponsor.
4. **Season 2 inventory.** If Chrome Yatra sells out, this map is the
   day-one inventory for the next machine — the 12-lot aesthetic model on a
   fresh bike, with these micro-surfaces as the expansion lots.
5. **Price discipline lesson.** ₹1,000 openings attracted nobody and
   undervalued the campaign. Scarcity pricing works; commodity pricing
   doesn't.
