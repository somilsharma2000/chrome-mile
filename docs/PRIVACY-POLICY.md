# Privacy Policy

> **Status: concept stage.** Last updated 11 October 2026.

Chrome Mile is a concept-stage campaign site. This page explains, plainly, what data this site does and does not handle.

## What this site does NOT do

- **No accounts, no server storage.** This is a static site. There is no login, no database, no form backend. Nothing you type here is transmitted or stored by the site itself.
- **No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party pixels.** See the [Cookie Policy](COOKIE-POLICY.md).
- **No sale or sharing of data.** No lists, no brokers, no partners.

## What actually happens when you register interest

The enquiry form composes a draft in **your own email client** (or copies it to your clipboard, your choice). The email goes to the campaign inbox only if you send it yourself. The site never transmits anything.

If you email the campaign inbox, the operator keeps your message (name, brand, work email, lot of interest, note) solely to reply to your enquiry and, if the campaign proceeds, to continue that conversation. You can withdraw at any time.

## Your data rights

- **Access / correction / deletion:** email the campaign inbox with the subject "Data deletion request" and your correspondence will be deleted or corrected promptly. Because the site stores nothing server-side, deletion applies to the email thread only.
- **Unsubscribe:** every campaign email now or in the future will carry an unsubscribe option; replying "stop" also ends all further emails. No bulk mailing exists today.
- **No automated decisions or profiling** are performed about you.

## Children

This site is a business-to-business campaign, not directed at anyone under 18, and no data is knowingly collected from minors. If any such data reaches the inbox, it is deleted on discovery or request.
