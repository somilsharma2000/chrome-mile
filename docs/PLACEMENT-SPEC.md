## Untouched — never offered
Chrome tank top, exhaust system, headlight, mirrors, number plate, lighting and controls. The
livery is composed as a whole; artwork that breaks the composition is declined at any budget.

## How verification works (per placement)
1. Measured template created on the confirmed vehicle (admin checklist §4).
2. Artwork approved in a studio mockup by the brand, in writing.
3. Professional installation, photographed — the photograph is the installation proof.
4. GPS-logged ride season with named logs and dated certificates — the log is the distance proof.
No deliverable is ever claimed before its evidence exists.
