# Chrome Yatra — Installation Readiness Checklist

**Status of every lot: CONCEPT — not yet installation-ready.**
This document tracks what must physically be verified before any lot can be marked
installation-ready. No lot ships on a good-looking concept mockup alone.

## 1. Physical measurement
- [ ] Placement zone measured on the actual machine (templates cut and test-fitted)
- [ ] Curvature and contour mapped for each lot
- [ ] Lot dimensions on the published map match the measured template

## 2. Material and surface compatibility
- [ ] Vinyl / adhesive spec approved for chrome and painted surfaces
- [ ] Test application on a hidden matching surface
- [ ] Heat, fuel-spill and weather behaviour confirmed for the spec

## 3. Safety and legal placement
- [ ] Placement does not obstruct lights, reflectors, number plate or controls
- [ ] Placement complies with motor-vehicle and local requirements
- [ ] No interference with rider visibility or machine operation

## 4. Artwork rights and approval
- [ ] Brand artwork usage rights confirmed in writing
- [ ] Artwork approved by the brand (colour, version, orientation)
- [ ] Artwork approved by the campaign (livery composition, law, decency)

## 5. Removal
- [ ] Removal method documented (heat + adhesive-safe process)
- [ ] Residue or surface-damage risk disclosed to the brand in writing
- [ ] Removal responsibility and cost assigned in the sponsor agreement

## 6. Sign-off
A lot is marked installation-ready only when sections 1-5 are complete and signed off
by the campaign owner. Until then, every site preview is a concept, not a promise.

**Current state:** All twelve lots are at concept stage. Measurement and test-fit
begin after the auction awards its winners, before any payment is collected in full.
