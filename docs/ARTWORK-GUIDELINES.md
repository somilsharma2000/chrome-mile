# Chrome Yatra — Artwork Guidelines

How to prepare your mark for the livery. Print-ready means vector; everything else costs quality on chrome.

## File formats
- **Required:** vector — AI, EPS or SVG, with fonts outlined/converted to curves.
- **Accepted as support:** PNG at 300 dpi minimum (for reference only; we redraw from vector when possible).
- **Not accepted:** JPG, screenshots, logos ripped from websites or PDFs.

## Colour
- Supply Pantone (PMS) codes, or CMYK values used for print. HEX is fine as a secondary reference.
- The livery sits on chrome and deep painted surfaces: high-contrast artwork reads best. We will flag low-contrast marks (e.g. silver on chrome) before production.

## Sizing and safe areas
- Each lot has a fixed published size (e.g. tank flank pair 12 × 4 cm). Artwork is scaled to fit; it is not cropped or stretched.
- Keep all critical elements 2 mm inside the lot boundary (safe area).
- Complex logos: we will recommend the simplified/stacked version if the full lock-up won't survive at lot size.

## Material and finish
- Premium cast vinyl, laminated matte or gloss — finish matched to the lot's surface.
- Chrome surfaces and the exhaust are never printed on (heat and chrome are unkind to vinyl); lots avoid them by design.

## Approval flow
1. You send assets + brand profile form (7 working days from signing).
2. We return a photo-accurate mockup of your mark on the bike within 5 working days.
3. One revision round included; approval or revision within 5 working days.
4. Approved mockup is signed off digitally, then printed and installed.

## What gets declined
- Artwork that breaks the livery's composition or clashes with claimed lots.
- Content that is defamatory, political, adult, or unlawful in India.
- Claims the campaign cannot stand behind ("official partner of Royal Enfield", etc.).
- Files too low-quality to print at the lot's size.

If we decline, we say why, and we refund anything already paid against that lot.
