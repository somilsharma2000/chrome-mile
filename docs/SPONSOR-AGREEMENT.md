- All payments via payment gateway to the campaign's registered account; digital receipts issued.

## 3. Campaign obligations
The campaign will, at its cost:
1. Print the Sponsor's approved artwork on premium vinyl to the lot's published size.
2. Install the decal at the published position within 30 days of the machine's delivery.
3. Deliver professional install photography within 7 days of installation.
4. Operate the touring season with GPS logging and deliver a per-ride certificate (date, route, distance) to the Sponsor within 7 days of each tour.
5. Grant the Sponsor usage rights to the campaign's ride imagery featuring the lot, for the Sponsor's own marketing, with mutual credit.
6. Deliver the closing report at season end: lots sold, kilometres ridden, certificates issued, archive links.

## 4. Sponsor obligations
The Sponsor will:
1. Deliver vector logo files (AI/EPS/SVG), brand colour codes and the completed brand profile form within 7 working days of signing.
2. Approve or request one revision of the placement mockup within 5 working days of receipt.
3. Not misrepresent the campaign in its own marketing (no claims of ownership of the campaign, no "official partner of Royal Enfield" claims).

## 5. Term
The lot license runs for the full campaign season from the machine's delivery date and ends on the published season close. Renewal terms are negotiated separately.

## 6. Refund and void
If the auction voids (any lot unsold at close), all payments and deposits are refunded in full within 7 working days. If the campaign cannot deliver install photography within 60 days of the machine's delivery, the Sponsor may claim a full refund of the 70% balance.

## 7. Legal
- This campaign is independent and is not affiliated with Royal Enfield or Eicher Motors. Both parties acknowledge this.
- Placement complies with the Motor Vehicles Act, Section 52 (no RC variance; nothing on number plate, lights or mirrors), and is declared to the vehicle insurer.
- Disputes: good-faith negotiation first; failing that, jurisdiction of the courts at [Founder's city], India.
