# Chrome Mile — Deliverables Schedule

What every lot winner receives, and when. Clock references: **D0** = machine
delivery date, **S0** = signing of the sponsor agreement.

| # | Deliverable | Due | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Placement mockup (photo-accurate) | S0 + 5 working days | After brand assets received |
| 2 | Mockup revision round (one included) | S0 + 10 working days | — |
| 3 | Install photography (professional, full lot) | D0 + 37 days (install ≤ 30 days, photos ≤ 7 days after) | Triggers the 70% payment |
| 4 | Launch livery photo set (full bike in completed livery) | D0 + 45 days | All twelve lots installed |
| 5 | Per-ride certificate (date, route, distance) | Within 7 days of every tour | GPS log attached to each |
| 6 | Ride photo update with sponsor's lot in frame | Monthly | When the bike is on tour |
| 7 | Archive access (public ride log, named and dated) | Continuous | Every tour published |
| 8 | Usage rights confirmation (imagery for sponsor marketing) | With install photography | Mutual credit applies |
| 9 | Season closing report (km ridden, certificates issued, archive index) | Season close | Published to all sponsors |

## Certificate format
Each ride certificate carries: ride date, start and end points, GPS distance
in km, the campaign seal, and the lots in frame that tour. Certificates are
issued per ride — not estimated, not projected.

## If the campaign falls behind
- Install photography more than 60 days late: the sponsor may claim a full refund of the 70% balance.
- Any tour not GPS-logged is not certified — there are no "trust me" rides.
- Missed monthly updates are logged in the closing report, not hidden.

Live delivery status is public on the campaign page. If we ever fall short,
it will be visible there before you have to ask.
