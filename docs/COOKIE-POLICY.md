# Cookie Policy

> **Status: concept stage.** Last updated 11 October 2026.

## The short version

**This site uses no cookies and no tracking of any kind.**

- No analytics. No advertising pixels. No session tracking. No fingerprinting. No third-party scripts — the site loads its own code, its own fonts and its own images, all served from this domain.
- The only thing stored on your device is **one browser-local note** (localStorage key `cm-notice-closed`) that remembers you closed the cookie notice, so it does not reappear. It never leaves your browser and contains nothing about you.
- The operator-only admin page (`/admin`) keeps the partnership-readiness checklist in browser localStorage as a convenience for the operator. It is not collected from visitors.

## Clearing it

Clearing site data in your browser (or browsing in a private window) removes the single note. Nothing else exists to remove.

If this site ever adds a measurement or consent-needing technology, this page and the site banner will change **before** it goes live.
