How it works

FROM CONCEPT TO VERIFIED LIVERY

Five stages, in public, with nothing skipped. We are at stage one today — and this page changes the moment each stage is actually reached.

01

Concept — where we are now

The livery, the five placements and the verification model are designed and published. Nothing is sold, no vehicle is secured, and no price is committed. Brands register interest — free, non-binding.

02

Vehicle partnership & measurement

A vehicle owner partnership is signed (the readiness checklist in our admin governs this). The machine is photographed and every placement is physically measured. Only then do placements stop being illustrative.

03

Pricing, auction & your approval

Pricing and dates are announced to registered brands first, with first refusal on your lot. If the auction runs, your artwork is approved in a studio mockup before anything touches the machine.

04

Installation, documented

Professional decal installation at the measured position and size, with install photography. Nothing is claimed as installed until the photographs exist.

05

The season rides, with proof

Every tour is GPS-logged: named ride logs, per-kilometre certificates and a public archive. Full usage rights to the imagery for your own marketing. Reporting is evidence, never estimates.

The partnership kit

WHAT EVERY PARTNER GETS

Concrete deliverables tied to your placement, each verified by its own evidence — proposed now, contracted on the sponsor agreement before installation.

Exclusive placement rights

One brand per placement for the full campaign season. No shared placements, no rotating slots.

Install photography

Professional shots of your mark fitted on the machine or gear — delivered after installation, because it isn't a deliverable until it exists.

GPS-verified ride proof

Tours are GPS-logged. You receive per-ride distance certificates and photo updates of your placement on the road.

Logo usage rights

The campaign's ride photos may be used in your own marketing, with mutual credit.

What you will never get here

Invented reach numbers, promised impressions, or a billboard claim. Exclusions are as explicit as deliverables: no co-branding, no number plates, lights or mirrors, no artwork that breaks the livery.

Built honest

TRANSPARENT BY CONSTRUCTION

Road-legal by design

Decals are small, bodywork-only placements — no alteration of the Registration Certificate particulars (Motor Vehicles Act, Section 52). Nothing goes on the number plate, lights, mirrors or windscreen. Installation conditions are part of the vehicle-owner agreement.

Insurance, taxes, consent — declared

Decal placement will be declared to the vehicle insurer, and sponsorship income reported and taxed as business income in India, with applicable TDS processed for brand accounting. All of it happens after the owner agreement is signed — not before.

Verified or it didn't happen

No deliverable is ever claimed before its evidence exists: install photography for installation, GPS logs for kilometres, certificates for the season. Concept imagery is always labelled as concept imagery.

Honesty in writing

This site states its own status plainly: concept stage, no vehicle secured, no live auction, no prices committed. When that changes, this section and the documents change first — with dates.

FAQ

ASKED STRAIGHT

Is the auction live?+

No. Chrome Mile is at concept stage: no vehicle partnership is signed, no placement is measured, and no auction is live. Registering brand interest is free and non-binding. The moment the vehicle is secured, pricing and dates are announced here and to registered brands first.

Is that your motorcycle in the photos?+

No — and the site never claims it is. The showroom uses a licensed concept photograph of a Continental GT 650 in the character of the target machine, labelled illustrative. When a real partnership machine is secured, verified photographs replace it.

What am I registering, then?+

A place in line. Your note of interest records the placement you want, and you get first refusal on it before pricing and dates go public. It is not a bid, a deposit, a booking or any claim on inventory — no money moves at concept stage.

What exactly would my brand get?+

Exclusive use of your placement for the campaign season: your mark installed at the measured size and position, professional install photography, GPS-verified ride certificates from every tour, and usage rights to the ride imagery for your own marketing. One brand per placement — no co-branding, no rotation.

Why hasn't the vehicle been secured yet?+

Because we don't pretend. Securing the right machine — a chrome cafe-racer whose owner will commit it to a documented touring season — takes a signed agreement covering access, consent, insurance and installation conditions. The readiness checklist for exactly that is public in the documents section.

Are the placement sizes and locations final?+

They are proposed, not measured. Sizes come from the machine's specification and are illustrative until physical measurement on the actual vehicle. Every studio export carries the label: not to scale, subject to vehicle access, measurement, material compatibility, safety and required approvals.

How is the livery kept beautiful?+

Every decal size is fixed in advance and the livery is designed as a whole — chrome surfaces, the exhaust, lighting and controls stay untouched. If a brand's artwork doesn't fit the composition, we say no.

Are decals on a vehicle even legal?+

Small bodywork decals on a private-registered vehicle don't alter Registration Certificate particulars (Motor Vehicles Act, Section 52), and placement will be declared to the insurer. Nothing is placed on the number plate, lights or mirrors. These conditions are part of the vehicle-owner agreement.

Is this affiliated with Royal Enfield?+

No. Chrome Mile is an independent concept campaign, not affiliated with or endorsed by Royal Enfield or Eicher Motors.
